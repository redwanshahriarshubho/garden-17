Gardening Tips

Watering Guide

Most houseplants prefer to dry out slightly between waterings. Stick your finger 1-2 inches into the soil - if it's dry, it's time to water!

Light Requirements

Match plants to your light conditions: low light (north-facing windows), medium light (east/west), or bright light (south-facing).

Seasonal Care

Reduce watering in winter when growth slows. Increase humidity during dry months by grouping plants together or using a humidifier.
